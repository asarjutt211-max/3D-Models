◆ The Idea

Not just to watch — to enjoy.

01

A gallery that never stops moving.

Instead of static images and videos, this site presents a collection of interactive 3D sculptures and animations. Everything renders live in the browser — no plugins, no downloads, no waiting.

02

Made to be played with.

Every model can be rotated, zoomed, and explored from any angle. Every animation responds to your touch and your movement. Nothing here is passive — the entire experience is yours to control.

03

Fun is part of the design.

These worlds aren't just for looking at — they're for playing with. Rotate a galaxy, pop a bubble, launch a comet, catch fireflies, solve a puzzle. Take your time, try things, and enjoy.

04

A showcase for what's possible.

This site exists as a demonstration — a live concept that shows how modern web technology can turn simple screens into small, entertaining worlds worth exploring.

05

Built to last, and to grow.

New models, new animations, and new ideas will keep being added. This is not a finished product — it's a living project that evolves over time.
